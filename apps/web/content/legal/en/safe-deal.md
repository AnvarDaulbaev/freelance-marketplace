# Safe Deal Rules

*This English version is provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Russian version prevails.*

## 1. Overview

1.1. Safe Deal protects both parties: the Client's payment is held by a licensed payment partner and is transferred to the Freelancer only after the work is accepted.

1.2. The Operator is not a bank or a payment institution and does not hold Users' funds. Funds are held and transferred by the payment partner under its own rules. The Operator transmits transfer instructions to the partner in accordance with these Rules.

## 2. Order Stages

| Status | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Awaiting Payment | The Client has selected a Freelancer and must pay for the Order |
| In Progress | The payment has been reserved; the Freelancer is performing the work |
| Under Review | The Freelancer has submitted the work; the Client is reviewing it |
| Revision Requested | The Client has returned the work with comments |
| Completed | The work has been accepted; the funds, less the commission, have been transferred to the Freelancer |
| Cancelled | The funds have been refunded to the Client |
| Dispute | The decision is made by a Moderator |

## 3. Acceptance of Work

3.1. After the work is submitted, the Client has **3 calendar days** to review it. Within this period, the Client accepts the work, returns it for revision with a description of the comments, or opens a dispute.

3.2. **If the Client takes none of these actions within the review period, the work is deemed automatically accepted**, and the funds are transferred to the Freelancer.

3.3. A request for revision must contain specific comments within the scope of the original assignment. Requirements beyond the scope of the assignment are placed as a new Order.

## 4. Cancellation

4.1. Before payment, either party may cancel the Order.

4.2. After payment, the Freelancer may withdraw from the Order — the funds are refunded to the Client in full.

4.3. After payment, the Client may not cancel the Order unilaterally. If the Freelancer has missed the deadline, is not responding, or is performing the work not in accordance with the assignment, the Client opens a dispute.

## 5. Disputes

5.1. Either party may open a dispute while the Order has the status "In Progress", "Under Review" or "Revision Requested". Automatic acceptance is suspended for the duration of the dispute.

5.2. The Moderator examines the assignment, the correspondence in the Order chat, and the submitted materials. The Moderator may request explanations from the parties. A party that does not respond within 3 business days risks a decision not in its favour.

5.3. Possible decisions:
- **in favour of the Client** — full refund of the funds; no commission is charged;
- **in favour of the Freelancer** — transfer of the funds to the Freelancer, less the commission;
- **partial refund** — part of the amount is refunded to the Client, and the remainder, less the commission, is transferred to the Freelancer.

5.4. The Moderator's decision is final within the Platform and is executed by the payment partner. The parties retain the right to go to court, but the Operator is not a defendant in a dispute between the parties to an Order.

## 6. Risks of Settlements outside the Platform

If the parties have agreed on direct payment, Safe Deal protection does not apply, and the Operator does not review such disputes or compensate losses. Offering payment outside the Platform violates the [Platform Rules](/en/legal/rules).

## 7. Withdrawal of Funds

7.1. The Freelancer withdraws the funds received using the methods available from the payment partner. The partner's withdrawal fee is shown before the transaction is confirmed.

7.2. Identity verification may be required for withdrawal. The Operator may suspend a withdrawal for the duration of a review on suspicion of fraud or of a violation of the legislation on countering the legalization (laundering) of criminal proceeds.
