# Platform Rules

*This English version is provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Russian version prevails.*

These Rules are binding on all Users. For a violation, a Moderator may hide materials, restrict account features, or block the account. Blocking does not release the User from obligations under Orders already started.

## 1. Prohibited from Being Posted or Ordered

- services prohibited by the laws of the Kyrgyz Republic or of the User's country;
- completing academic, examination or qualification work on behalf of another person;
- artificial inflation of reviews, ratings, votes, followers or views;
- mass mailings (spam), collection of personal data without consent;
- creation and distribution of malware, hacking, circumvention of security measures, DDoS;
- production of forged documents, forgery of signatures and seals;
- content that infringes copyright and related rights, or trademark rights;
- pornography, materials involving minors, promotion of violence, extremism, terrorism or drugs;
- financial pyramid schemes, unlicensed gambling, cash-out services and money laundering services;
- assignments that require payment by the Freelancer (entry fees, "test" payments).

## 2. Communication and Settlements

2.1. **Settlements for Orders found on the Platform are made only through the Platform.** Offering to pay for work directly, and sharing card numbers, wallets, phone numbers, messenger handles and other contact details before an Order is created, are prohibited.

2.2. Correspondence regarding an Order is conducted in the Order chat. Only this correspondence is accepted as evidence in a dispute.

2.3. Insults, threats, discrimination, and pressure on the other party to change a review are prohibited.

## 3. Accounts

3.1. One person or one organization — one account.

3.2. Transferring, selling or renting out an account is prohibited.

3.3. Impersonating another person and providing false information about qualifications and experience are prohibited.

## 4. Projects and Proposals

4.1. A Project must contain a clear description of the task. The budget, if specified, must be realistic.

4.2. A Proposal must relate to a specific Project. Mass template proposals may be hidden.

4.3. Publishing the same Project multiple times is prohibited.

## 5. Reviews

5.1. A review may be left only for a completed Order.

5.2. A review must describe a genuine work experience. Paid reviews, as well as reviews from one's own or affiliated accounts, are prohibited.

## 6. Measures for Violations

| Violation | Possible Measures |
|---|---|
| Contacts and payment outside the Platform | warning, blocking if repeated |
| Prohibited services | removal of materials, blocking |
| Fraud, multiple accounts | blocking of all associated accounts, suspension of payouts for the duration of a review |
| Insults, threats | warning, restriction of features, blocking |

A User may appeal a Moderator's decision by writing to [EMAIL] within 30 days. The appeal is reviewed by the Operator.

## 7. Complaints

Any User may report a Project or a User using the "Report" button. Complaints are reviewed by Moderators in the order received.
