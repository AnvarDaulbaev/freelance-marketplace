# Cookie Policy

*This English version is provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Russian version prevails.*

## 1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in the browser.

## 2. Which Cookies We Use

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `fm_session` | account login session (httpOnly, inaccessible to scripts) | 7 days |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | selected interface language | 1 year |
| `cookie-notice-ok` (localStorage) | remembers that the cookie notice has been closed | indefinite |

All of the cookies and localStorage entries listed are strictly necessary for the operation of the Platform, so no separate consent is required for them.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. If they are introduced, we will update this Policy and will request consent before setting them.

## 3. How to Manage Cookies

Cookies can be deleted or blocked in the browser settings. Without the session cookie, it is impossible to log in to the account.
