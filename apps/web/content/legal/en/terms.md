- **User** — an individual who has reached 18 years of age, an individual entrepreneur, or a legal entity registered on the Platform.
- **Client** — a User who posts Projects and orders services.
- **Freelancer** — a User who offers services and performs Orders.
- **Project** — a description of an assignment posted by a Client.
- **Proposal** — a Freelancer's offer stating the price and timeframe for performing a Project.
- **Order** — an arrangement between a Client and a Freelancer that arises once a Proposal is selected.
- **Moderator** — an employee authorized by the Operator who monitors compliance with the rules and reviews disputes.
- **Safe Deal** — a settlement procedure under which the Client's payment is held by a payment partner until the work is accepted.
