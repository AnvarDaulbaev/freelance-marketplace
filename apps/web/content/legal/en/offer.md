# Public Offer for the Provision of Platform Services

*This English version is provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Russian version prevails.*

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME], Tax ID (INN) [TAX ID (INN)] (hereinafter, the "Operator"), offers any User to enter into a contract for the provision of Platform services on the terms of this offer, in accordance with the provisions of the Civil Code of the Kyrgyz Republic on public offers.

## 1. Acceptance

1.1. Acceptance of the offer is effected by registering on the Platform and ticking the consent checkbox. For Orders created before the offer is amended, the version in effect on the date the Order was created applies.

1.2. Acceptance constitutes full and unconditional acceptance of the terms of the offer, the [User Agreement](/en/legal/terms), the [Platform Rules](/en/legal/rules) and the [Safe Deal Rules](/en/legal/safe-deal).

## 2. Subject Matter

2.1. The Operator provides Users with services of access to the Platform: posting Projects and Proposals, messaging, arranging settlements through payment partners, and reviewing disputes.

2.2. Services for Clients and registration are free of charge. The Operator's remuneration is a commission withheld from the Order amount payable to the Freelancer.

## 3. Platform Commission

3.1. The commission is charged to the Freelancer for each successfully completed Order. The rate is determined by the amount of the specific Order and applies to the entire Order amount:

| Order Amount | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| less than KGS 30,000 | 20% |
| KGS 30,000 or more, but less than KGS 300,000 | 12% |
| KGS 300,000 or more | 7% |

3.2. The rate is fixed at the time the Order is created and does not change until the Order is completed. The commission amount and the amount receivable are shown to the Freelancer before a Proposal is submitted and on the Order page.

3.3. The commission is withheld when funds are transferred to the Freelancer after the Order is accepted. If an Order is cancelled with a full refund to the Client, no commission is charged. In the event of a partial refund, the commission is calculated on the amount actually transferred to the Freelancer, at the rate fixed in the Order.

3.4. Payment partners' fees for depositing and withdrawing funds are not included in the Platform commission and are shown before the transaction is confirmed.

3.5. For settlements in other currencies, the thresholds are determined by the KGS equivalent at the exchange rate shown on the Platform on the date the Order is created.

## 4. Settlement Procedure

4.1. Settlements are made through licensed payment partners. The Operator does not accept Users' funds into its own accounts, except for its commission.

4.2. The procedure for reserving, transferring and refunding funds is set out in the [Safe Deal Rules](/en/legal/safe-deal).

4.3. The Freelancer independently pays taxes on the income received. The Operator is not the Freelancer's tax agent unless expressly required by law.

## 5. Liability and Disputes

5.1. The liability of the parties and the dispute resolution procedure are determined by the [User Agreement](/en/legal/terms).

5.2. The Operator's liability under this contract is limited to the amount of the commission received for the Order to which the claim relates.

## 6. Term and Amendment

6.1. The contract remains in effect until the User's account is deleted.

6.2. The Operator may amend the offer, including the commission rates, by publishing the new version at least 10 days before it takes effect. Changes do not apply to Orders created earlier.
